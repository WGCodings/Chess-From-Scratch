# Feedback and contributing

Chess From Scratch is new, and it gets better with every bit of feedback. If something confused you, broke, or was missing, please tell us. That is the most useful help there is.

## Tell us what you think

Click **Feedback** in the app's **☰** menu, or go to the [issues page on GitHub](https://github.com/WGCodings/Chess-From-Scratch/issues), and click **New issue**. You need a free GitHub account.

A few lines are enough:

- **Something broke?** Write what you did, what you expected, and what happened. A screenshot helps a lot. If it is about your engine, click **Save** in the app and attach the `.cfs` file.
- **You got stuck?** Tell us where. If the guide or the app didn't explain something well, that is our mistake, not yours.
- **An idea?** New blocks, better explanations, anything. Say what you want to do and why.

Before you write, have a quick look if someone already asked the same. If so, add a 👍 or a comment there.

## Share your blocks

Made a block you are proud of? Right-click it, pick **Export block file**, and attach the `.block.rs` file to an issue. Good blocks may become examples in the app.

## Help with the code

Chess From Scratch is open source. Its code is on [GitHub](https://github.com/WGCodings/Chess-From-Scratch). If you want to fix something yourself:

1. Open an issue first, so we can agree on the idea.
2. Fork the repository, make your change, and run the tests.
3. Open a pull request.

The file `CONTRIBUTING.md` in the repository has the details.

## Licenses, in short

- **Your engine is yours.** Every exported engine comes with the MIT license and your name. You can share it, change it, enter it in tournaments, or sell it. Just keep the MIT notice for the parts that came from the app's templates.
- **The app itself** is open source under the GNU AGPL v3. You may use, study and change it. If you run a changed version as a website, you must share your changes under the same license.
